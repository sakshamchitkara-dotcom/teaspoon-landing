        Questions at the counter

        How sweet is 50%?

        Half the syrup of a standard drink. If you usually find boba too sweet, start at 25% or 50% and adjust next time.

        Do the drinks have caffeine?

        Anything made with black, green, or oolong tea does. Brown sugar pearl milk has no tea, so it is a good late-night pick.

        What are tapioca pearls made of?

        Starch from the cassava root, cooked until chewy and soaked in a light syrup.
