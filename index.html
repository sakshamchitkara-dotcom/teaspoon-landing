    A South Bay tea break

    San Jose runs on long commutes, late study sessions, and weekend errands. A good cup of tea should fit into all of them.

    This valley used to be orchards before it was chips, and it still rewards things grown and made with care. We brew tea in small batches through the day, cook pearls often so they stay soft, and let you set the sugar yourself.

    Stop in on the way to class, after a shift, or with the whole group chat. Stay as long as you like.
